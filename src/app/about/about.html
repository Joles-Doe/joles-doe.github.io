1 / WHO AM I?

Curious by nature

I’m Scott, a third-year student at Bournemouth University studying Games Software Engineering. My love for games brought me to the course, and I’ve always been fascinated by how games work. The technology, ingenuity, and creativity behind them drives me to strive for greater heights.

I’m willing to try anything. I’ve tinkered with computers, created my own game engines, and even driven trucks three times my size. I like to commit myself to new challenges, build my drive, and take useful skills with me into whatever comes next.
