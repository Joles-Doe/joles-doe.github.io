UNITY · C#

Untethered

A metroidvania vertical slice demo made in a team of nine using Unity.

Overview

During my second year, I worked with a team of nine to create a vertical slice for a metroidvania-style game in Unity. The project gave us the chance to build a focused section of a larger game and develop its core player experience.

My contribution

I worked as a general programmer, with responsibility for creating the player movement system. I also acted as a point of contact for designers who wanted programming insight, helping them understand how their ideas could be approached and implemented.

Movement

I looked at movement in metroidvania games, including Hollow Knight, to understand what players of the genre expect and enjoy. Rather than use Unity’s standard player controller, I built a custom one so I could fine-tune the movement physics for our game.

What I learned

Working in a larger team gave me a useful insight into how a game project is coordinated across different roles. I also learned to work through the communication challenges that can come with a bigger group, and to keep design and programming aligned as the project developed.
