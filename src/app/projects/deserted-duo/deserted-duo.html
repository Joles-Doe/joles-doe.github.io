Deserted Duo

A small game demo made in 48 hours for the 2024 BFX Game Jam. Built around the theme “connection”.

GH GitHub

Overview

In my second year at Bournemouth University, I participated in a team of five for the 2024 BFX Game Jam. The theme was “connection,” so we made a local multiplayer game around working together. In 48 hours, our team won first place, along with awards for Best Mechanics and Best 3D Art.

My contribution

I was the programming lead, responsible for implementing the game mechanics and helping the different systems work together. That meant coordinating with the rest of the team as their parts of the game came together.

The keybind mechanic

Players begin with movement controls only. To gain abilities such as jumping, they pick up keybinds in the level and swap them for controls they already have. I used enumerators to track which actions each player had assigned, then made the movement script check the keybind block for each input.

Checkpoints and respawning

Because the game used ragdoll physics, moving a fallen player back to a checkpoint by changing its position caused problems. I built a respawn flow that creates a new player from a prefab at the saved checkpoint and restores the information they need. Checkpoint triggers record the player's location as they reach each point.

What I learned

The game jam gave me a first-hand look at a team game-development pipeline. As programming lead, I saw how each person's work fit together and had to solve problems at the boundaries between systems. It was a valuable experience in building, communicating, and integrating work under a tight deadline.
